How Findaro search works

Suggestions and in-page results are fetched live from the free Wikipedia API and labeled as such — they are encyclopedia articles, not whole-web results, and Findaro never invents results or summaries. Recent searches and favorites are stored only in this browser; clear them any time from the search box or tool cards.
